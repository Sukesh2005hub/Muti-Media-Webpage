🎬 TOP 11 Most-Watched Indian Trailers in 24 Hours

1. Salaar: Part 1 – Ceasefire - 113.2 million views 😲

Salaar: Part 1 – Ceasefire is a 2023 Telugu-language action thriller directed by Prashanth Neel. The film stars Prabhas and Prithviraj Sukumaran. The story follows Deva and Varadha in the fictional city-state of Khansaar.

2. K.G.F: Chapter 2 - 106.5 million views 😱

KGF: Chapter 2 is a 2022 Kannada-language period action film directed by Prashanth Neel. It is the sequel to KGF: Chapter 1. The film stars Yash, Sanjay Dutt, Raveena Tandon and Srinidhi Shetty. The story continues Rocky's journey in the Kolar Gold Fields.

3. Pushpa 2: The Rule - 104.2 million views 😵

Pushpa 2: The Rule is a 2024 Telugu-language action drama directed by Sukumar. The film stars Allu Arjun, Rashmika Mandanna and Fahadh Faasil. The story continues Pushpa Raj's journey as a red-sandalwood smuggler.

4. Peddi - 85.72 million views 🔥

Peddi is a 2026 Telugu-language sports action drama directed by Buchi Babu Sana. The film stars Ram Charan, Janhvi Kapoor, Shiva Rajkumar, Jagapathi Babu and Divyenndu. The story is set in rural Andhra Pradesh and follows Peddi, a talented sportsman who becomes involved in a struggle for his village and community.

5. Adipurush - 74 million views 🏹

Adipurush is a 2023 Indian mythological action film directed by Om Raut. The film stars Prabhas, Kriti Sanon, Saif Ali Khan and Sunny Singh. It is inspired by the Hindu epic Ramayana and follows the story of Raghav and his journey against Lankesh.

6. Salaar (Release Trailer) - 72.2 million views 💥

Salaar Release Trailer is the release trailer for Salaar: Part 1 – Ceasefire. It features Prabhas, Prithviraj Sukumaran, Shruti Haasan and other members of the cast. The trailer presents the film's action, characters and world of Khansaar.

7. Animal - 71.4 million views 🐺

Animal is a 2023 Hindi-language action drama directed by Sandeep Reddy Vanga. The film stars Ranbir Kapoor, Anil Kapoor, Rashmika Mandanna and Bobby Deol. The story follows Ranvijay and his complicated relationship with his father Balbir.

8. Game Changer - 62 million views ⚡

Game Changer is a 2025 Telugu-language political action thriller directed by S. Shankar. The film stars Ram Charan, Kiara Advani, Anjali and S. J. Suryah. The story follows an honest IAS officer who enters politics and fights corruption.

9. Hari Hara Veera Mallu (HHVM) - 59.44 million views ⚔️

Hari Hara Veera Mallu is a 2025 Telugu-language historical action-adventure film. It stars Pawan Kalyan, Nidhhi Agerwal and Bobby Deol. The story is set during the Mughal period and follows Veera Mallu on a mission involving the Koh-i-Noor.

10. Dunki - 58.57 million views ❤️

Dunki is a 2023 Hindi-language comedy-drama directed by Rajkumar Hirani. The film stars Shah Rukh Khan, Taapsee Pannu, Boman Irani and Vicky Kaushal. The story follows a group of friends and their journey connected with immigration and their determination to reach another country.

11. Sky Force - 57.7 million views ✈️

Sky Force is a 2025 Hindi-language war drama directed by Sandeep Kewlani and Abhishek Anil Kapur. The film stars Akshay Kumar, Veer Pahariya, Sara Ali Khan and Nimrat Kaur. The story is centred around India's airstrike at the Sargodha airbase during the 1965 Indo-Pakistani war.
